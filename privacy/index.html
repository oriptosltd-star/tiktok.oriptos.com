Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026 • Oriptos Ltd

1. Introduction

Oriptos Ltd operates Oriptos TikTok Planner at tiktok.oriptos.com — a service that lets you schedule, queue and publish videos to your TikTok account through TikTok's official API. We take your privacy seriously, and all integrations with third-party networks, specifically TikTok for Developers, follow strict industry privacy standards. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address, and store your password in securely hashed form.

TikTok information. When you connect your TikTok account via official OAuth authentication, we receive permission-scoped data including your TikTok open ID, username, display name and profile avatar, together with the authorization tokens necessary to post content on your behalf under the video.upload and video.publish scopes you approve.

Your videos and scheduling data. We store the video files you upload in cloud object storage until they are published or you delete them, along with the scheduling metadata you set — captions, chosen accounts, dates, times and timezones — and the status of each queued post.

Technical logs. We keep basic technical logs (such as request times, error records and publish attempts) so we can operate the service, diagnose failures and keep accounts secure.

3. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your data to operate the service:

  • To authenticate your account into Oriptos TikTok Planner and keep you signed in.
  • To schedule, queue, and publish the video content you approve to your TikTok account, at the times you choose.
  • To display metrics, post status, and verification feedback inside your dashboard.
  • To respond to your support and data requests, and to protect the service against abuse.

4. What We Never Do

  • We do NOT store your TikTok password. Sign-in always happens on TikTok's own screens through official OAuth.
  • We never sell, rent, or transfer your personal or TikTok data to third-party data brokers or unauthorized entities.
  • We never post anything to your TikTok account that you did not queue or schedule yourself.
  • We do not use your videos or account data for advertising, and we run no advertising trackers on this site.

5. How We Share Information

We share data only where it is necessary to run the service:

  • Service providers that host the application, database and file storage on our behalf, under their own security and privacy obligations.
  • TikTok, when we publish a video at your direction through the official Content Posting API, and when TikTok provides your account details back to us under the permissions you granted.
  • Legal requirements, if we are required by law to disclose information.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

We use only the browser storage needed to keep you signed in and to operate your session securely. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

7. Data Security and Retention

All OAuth access tokens are securely stored using industry-grade encryption. We keep your uploaded video files until they are published or you delete them from your queue, and your scheduling records while your account is active. If you delete your account, we erase your profile, tokens, queue and stored files as described in our Data Deletion Instructions, keeping only the minimal records we must retain for legal or security reasons.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can access and correct your account information from your dashboard. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time via the Connected Accounts section of our dashboard or directly through your TikTok security settings — disconnecting destroys your stored authorization tokens. You may also request full erasure of your data at any time; see our Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at privacy@oriptos.com.
