Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2026 • Oriptos Ltd

1. Introduction: Oriptos Ltd operates tiktok.oriptos.com. We take your privacy seriously and ensure all integrations with third-party networks, specifically TikTok for Developers, follow strict industry privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect: When you connect your TikTok account via official OAuth authentication, we receive permission-scoped data including your TikTok username, display name, profile avatar, and authorization tokens necessary to post content on your behalf (such as video.upload and video.publish).

3. How We Use Data: We strictly use your TikTok credentials and tokens to:

  • Authenticate your account into the Oriptos Social Engine.
  • Schedule, queue, and publish user-approved video content to your TikTok account.
  • Display metrics, post status, and verification feedback.

4. Data Security: We do NOT store your TikTok passwords. All OAuth access tokens are securely stored using industry-grade encryption. We never sell, rent, or transfer your personal or TikTok data to third-party data brokers or unauthorized entities.

5. User Rights & Data Erasure: You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time via the Settings modal in our dashboard or directly through your TikTok Security settings. For full data erasure requests, please refer to our Data Deletion Instructions.
