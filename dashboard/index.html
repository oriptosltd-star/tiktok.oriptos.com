1. Introduction: Oriptos Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates tiktok.oriptos.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected via our application and TikTok for Developers integration.

2. Information We Collect: When you connect your TikTok account via official OAuth authentication, we receive permission-scoped data including your TikTok username, display name, profile avatar, and authorization tokens necessary to post content on your behalf (such as video.upload and video.publish).

3. Use of Information: We strictly use your TikTok credentials and tokens to:

  • Authenticate your account into the Oriptos Social Engine.
  • Schedule, queue, and publish user-approved video content to your TikTok account.
  • Display metrics, post status, and verification feedback.

4. Data Storage & Security: We do NOT store your TikTok passwords. All OAuth access tokens are securely stored using industry-grade encryption. We never sell, rent, or transfer your personal or TikTok data to third-party data brokers or unauthorized entities.

5. Data Deletion & Revocation: You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time via the Settings modal or directly through your TikTok Security settings. To request full deletion of your user account and stored metadata, visit our Data Deletion Instructions.
