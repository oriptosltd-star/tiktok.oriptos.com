User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with TikTok Developer Policies and GDPR

Oriptos Ltd respects your right to delete your personal data and revoke TikTok access tokens. If you wish to delete your data associated with our app, you have two options:

Option 1: In-App Revocation (Instant)

You can remove your TikTok connection at any time by navigating to your Dashboard → Connected Accounts and clicking "Disconnect". This instantly wipes your stored authorization tokens from our database.

Open Connected Accounts

Option 2: Direct Request to Data Protection Officer

You can email our data compliance team with your TikTok username. We will permanently erase all your logs, scheduled queues, and user profile data within 48 hours.

Email: privacy@oriptos.com or oriptosltd@gmail.com
