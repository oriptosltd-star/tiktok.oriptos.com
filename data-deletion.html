User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 28, 2026 • In accordance with TikTok Developer Policies and GDPR

Oriptos Ltd respects your right to delete your personal data and revoke TikTok access tokens. If you wish to delete your data associated with Oriptos TikTok Planner, you have two options:

Option 1: In-App Revocation (Instant)

You can remove your TikTok connection at any time by navigating to your Dashboard → Connected Accounts and clicking "Disconnect". This instantly wipes your stored authorization tokens from our database. You can also revoke our access at any time from your TikTok account's security settings.

Open Connected Accounts

Option 2: Email Request for Full Erasure

To delete your entire Oriptos TikTok Planner account and all associated data, email us from your account email address (or include your TikTok username) and ask for full erasure:

Email: privacy@oriptos.com

What Gets Deleted

On a full erasure request we permanently delete:

  • Your Oriptos TikTok Planner account and profile (name and email address).
  • Your stored TikTok authorization tokens — these are destroyed, so the service can no longer access or publish to your TikTok account.
  • Your queued and scheduled posts, together with the video files you uploaded that are still held in our storage.

Videos that have already been published to TikTok are posts on TikTok's platform; deleting your Planner data does not remove them. You can delete those posts directly in TikTok at any time.

What May Be Retained

We may retain minimal records — such as basic security and transaction logs — only where needed to meet legal obligations, prevent abuse, or resolve disputes. These records are kept separate from the service, are never used for marketing, and are deleted when they are no longer required.

Timeframe

Disconnecting in the dashboard takes effect instantly. Full erasure requests are completed within 30 days of receiving and verifying your request, and we will confirm by email once your data has been erased.

Questions

For anything about your data, contact privacy@oriptos.com. See also our Privacy Policy for how we collect and use data.
